Delete your Glame account

Last updated: 20 November 2026

You can delete your Glame account and its data at any time, whether you are a client or an artist.

In the app

  1. Open Glame and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@glameapp.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Glame account”. We will confirm it’s you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email, mobile number and sign-in details.
  • For artists: your profile, photos, portfolio, services and working hours.
  • Your reviews, notifications and saved devices for push notifications.

What we may keep

Records of bookings and payments that financial and legal rules require us to keep, for as long as those rules require. We keep them only for that purpose. See our Privacy Policy.

Questions? Email support@glameapp.com.
