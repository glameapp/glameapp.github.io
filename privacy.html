Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Glame (“we”, “us”) runs the Glame app, which connects clients with independent makeup artists and hairstylists in the GCC. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, mobile number, country and whether you are a client or an artist.
  • Artist profiles: business name, bio, area, studio address, services, prices, working hours, profile photo, portfolio photos and, if added, an Instagram username. These are shown publicly in the app.
  • Bookings: the service, date and time, location type, the address you give for home visits, and any notes.
  • Gifts: your friend’s name and mobile number and your message, so she can claim the gift.
  • Payments: amounts and payment status. Card details are entered on Tap Payments’ secure page; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Reviews and reports you write.
  • Saved artists: the artists you save, so you can find them again. Only you see this list.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send booking notifications to your phone.
  • Ad counts: how many times each sponsored banner is shown or tapped, as daily totals that are not linked to you.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as sign-in sessions and error logs.

How we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To show artists to clients and to make, manage and remind you of bookings.
  • To take deposits and gift payments, issue refunds and pay artists.
  • To verify mobile numbers by SMS for gifts.
  • To keep the app safe, handle reports and prevent fraud.
  • To send service emails and notifications about your bookings and gifts (for example, a new request, a confirmation, a cancellation or a reminder). We do not send marketing without your consent.
  • To show sponsored banners from other companies, marked “Sponsored”. We do not share your personal data with advertisers, and tapping a banner opens the advertiser’s own site, which has its own policy.

Who sees your information

  • Artists you book see your name, booking details and the address you give for home visits. Your phone number is shown to the artist, and the artist’s number to you, only once the booking is confirmed.
  • Clients see an artist’s public profile. They do not see the artist’s email.
  • Service providers that run parts of Glame for us: Supabase (database and hosting), Tap Payments (payments), Resend (booking emails), Google Firebase (phone notifications) and our SMS provider. They may only use the data to provide their service.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your profile and personal details. We may keep booking and payment records for as long as financial and legal rules require.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your details in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@glameapp.com.

We handle personal data in line with the data protection laws that apply in the GCC, including Bahrain’s Personal Data Protection Law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that people only see what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

Children

Glame is for people aged 18 and over, or younger users with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions? Email support@glameapp.com.
