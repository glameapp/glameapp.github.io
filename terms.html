2. Accounts

  • You must give accurate details and keep your password private.

3. Bookings

  • A booking request is confirmed only when the artist accepts it.
  • Prices are set by each artist in the currency of her country, and are shown before you book.
  • Home-visit fees, where offered, are added to the price.

4. Deposits, cancellations and refunds

  • Some bookings need a deposit paid online when you book. The rest is paid to the artist on the day.
  • If the artist declines or cancels, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • If you cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • If you cancel later than that, or don’t attend, the deposit is kept by the artist.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show them.

5. Gifts

  • You can pay for a service and send it to a friend by mobile number. She claims it after confirming that number by SMS.
  • A gift is valid for 12 months. You can cancel it for a full refund until it is booked.
  • Once booked, the deposit and cancellation rules above apply to the gift booking. If the booking is cancelled in time, the gift can be booked again.

6. Artists

  • Artists are responsible for their own taxes.

7. Reviews and content

  • Don’t post anything offensive, false, or that you don’t have the right to share.
